Magniscan
What We Built

A security scanning SaaS for citizen developers, solo founders and makers who built their app with AI tools like Lovable, Bolt or Replit and have no idea what vulnerabilities they may have accidentally shipped.

The Details

A free passive scan, no login needed, plus a deep active scan powered by OWASP ZAP — checking for exposed API keys, database credentials, open server ports, malware flags and data breaches. Every finding translated into plain English with a paste-ready fix prompt.

The Build

Built end-to-end in a matter of weeks, with Claude as the primary development partner. Stripe billing, domain verification, async scan architecture and a shareable results page — a fully featured SaaS product shipped at speed without a traditional engineering team.

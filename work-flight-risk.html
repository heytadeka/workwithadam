Flight Risk

Launching an e-bike brand from zero. Paid ads, email, and audience building — all at once.

5.6x Meta ROAS
15x Google ROAS
1000s early email subscribers

The Brief

Flight Risk came in as a brand-new player in the Australian e-bike market. No existing audience, no established brand recognition, no email list. The challenge: build awareness, drive early sales, and create a subscriber base that could fuel long-term growth — all simultaneously, and efficiently.

What We Did

The strategy was multi-channel from day one — because launching a brand means you need reach, retargeting, and retention working together, not in sequence.

  • Built and launched Meta campaigns across awareness, consideration, and conversion objectives
  • Developed custom and lookalike audiences based on early purchaser data and competitor interests
  • Ran TikTok campaigns targeting active lifestyle audiences and urban commuters
  • Set up Google Search and Shopping campaigns capturing high-intent buyers
  • Launched a pre-launch EDM campaign, driving thousands of early subscribers ahead of the store going live
  • Optimised Google My Business to drive local discovery and foot traffic

The Creative Approach

E-bike creative needs to do two things: make the product look desirable and answer the practical objections (range, price, reliability) that hold buyers back. The creative strategy led with lifestyle — freedom, speed, commuting without the sweat — and followed up with spec-driven content for retargeting.

Lookalike audiences were built from email subscribers and early purchasers, keeping acquisition costs low as the brand grew.

The Results

Meta delivered a 5.6x ROAS in the launch period. Google — capturing the high-intent search traffic — hit a 15x ROAS. The EDM campaign built a meaningful early subscriber base before the brand was even fully live, giving Flight Risk a warm audience to launch into.

The brand went from zero to traction without the slow burn that kills most new ecommerce launches.

Key Takeaway

"Launch campaigns work when you treat email, paid social, and search as a system — not separate channels. Flight Risk is the proof."

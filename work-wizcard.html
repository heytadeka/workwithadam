WizCard Hungary

Launching an NFC technology startup and landing partnerships with some of Hungary's biggest brand names — fast.

6 major brand partnerships secured
B2B enterprise-level clients
NFC technology category created
Key partnerships secured
BMW Hungary KFC Hungary ACCOR Hotels STIHL OPEL GARMIN

The Brief

WizCard is an NFC-based digital business card and customer engagement platform. The goal: launch the brand in Hungary, establish credibility fast, and sign enterprise-level clients who would validate the technology and create case studies for further growth.

The challenge with any B2B tech product is that credibility comes before clients, but clients create credibility. You have to break that loop somehow.

The Strategy

Rather than going broad, the strategy was to target specific industries where NFC technology had clear, demonstrable value — hospitality, automotive, QSR, and outdoor/lifestyle brands — and build a sales and marketing approach tailored to each sector.

  • Developed WizCard's brand identity and positioning as a B2B SaaS/hardware solution
  • Built targeted outreach campaigns to enterprise decision-makers across hospitality, automotive, and retail
  • Created case-study-ready pilot programmes to give prospects a low-risk entry point
  • Led sales conversations and partnership negotiations with major brand contacts
  • Developed use-case content showing exactly how NFC improved reviews, subscriber collection, and social growth for each industry vertical
  • Built the brand's LinkedIn presence and B2B content strategy

What NFC Solves

The value proposition was clear once the use cases were mapped: for a hotel like ACCOR, NFC cards eliminate friction on review collection. For a brand like BMW, they enable seamless lead capture at events and showrooms. For KFC, they can drive loyalty app installs and social follows at the point of sale.

Once the pitch was framed around each client's specific problem rather than the technology itself, the conversion rate improved dramatically.

The Results

Six major enterprise partnerships secured — BMW Hungary, KFC Hungary, ACCOR Hotels, STIHL, OPEL, and GARMIN. WizCard went from a startup with a product to a credible B2B brand with enterprise logos on its website. That proof of concept opened the door to the next tier of growth.

Key Takeaway

"B2B marketing is just B2C marketing where the buyer needs more permission to say yes. Give them a reason, remove the risk, and show them what it looks like on the other side. That's true whether you're selling clothes or NFC cards to BMW."

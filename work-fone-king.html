Fone King

"Free Screensaver Day" — an offline activation that brought hundreds of customers through the door, powered by digital thinking.

100s customers in-store (Miranda)
0→ concept to activation
IRL digital + physical connected

The Brief

Fone King is a retail phone accessories and repair brand with physical store locations. The challenge: in a market dominated by online shopping and digital ads, how do you drive genuine foot traffic and create a store experience people actually talk about?

The brief was to find an activation concept that could bridge digital marketing with physical retail — something that would bring customers in, create buzz, and build brand warmth.

The Idea: Free Screensaver Day

The concept was simple and deliberately so. Fone King would offer a free custom screensaver installation to any customer who came into the Miranda store on a single designated day.

Simple offers work. Freebies drive foot traffic. The key was in the execution — turning a small, low-cost offer into an event worth showing up for.

  • Developed the activation concept from brief to execution
  • Built the digital campaign to drive awareness and intent before the event day
  • Targeted local audiences via Meta — radius targeting around the Miranda location
  • Created social content and organic posts to build pre-event buzz
  • Managed in-store coordination to handle the volume of customers on the day
  • Captured content and customer data during the activation for follow-up campaigns

The Thinking Behind It

Most retail brands treat their physical stores as separate from their digital marketing. The store does its thing. The ads do their thing. They rarely talk to each other.

Free Screensaver Day was built on the opposite premise: the digital campaign exists to fill the store. The store experience builds the relationship. The data from the store feeds the next digital campaign. One loop, not two separate channels.

The Result

Hundreds of engaged customers came through the Miranda store on activation day. Long queues. Social sharing. Local word of mouth. The kind of in-store energy that money can't easily buy — but smart activation thinking can generate.

More than the foot traffic: the campaign proved that a digital-first mindset can power physical retail outcomes, not just online ones.

Key Takeaway

"The best retail activations aren't about the freebie. They're about giving people a reason to show up, and then making showing up worth it. Digital gets them there. The experience keeps them."

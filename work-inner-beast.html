Inner Beast
Clothing Co.

157% increase in online sales in six months. The kind of result that happens when you stop guessing and start testing properly.

157% increase in online sales (6 months)
6.1x META ROAS
3.83% conversion rate
+37% increase in average order value

The Brief

Inner Beast Clothing Co. had a good product and a loyal core following — but their paid ads weren't scaling. Sales were inconsistent, average order values were low, and the Meta campaigns weren't generating the kind of returns that justified increasing spend.

The goal: build a paid media system that could scale, drive more revenue, and improve the economics of each transaction.

What We Did

The strategy focused on Meta and Google — both rebuilt from the ground up with proper audience architecture, creative testing, and funnel structure.

  • Rebuilt Meta campaign structure across cold, warm, and hot audiences with clear separation
  • Developed a creative testing framework to identify top-performing ad concepts quickly
  • Introduced bundle and upsell messaging to lift average order value — targeting existing customers with higher-value offers
  • Google campaigns targeted high-intent brand and category search terms
  • Retargeting sequences tailored to where customers were in the buying journey
  • Regular creative refresh to prevent ad fatigue in a niche audience

The Creative Approach

Fitness and streetwear is a crowded space. Standing out means leaning into identity — not just product shots. The creative strategy for Inner Beast led with the community and lifestyle, using real customer content and aspirational imagery to build desire before the conversion ask.

The retargeting layer was more direct: product-focused, offer-led, with clear urgency where appropriate.

The Results

In six months: online sales up 157%. META ROAS of 6.1 — well above the industry benchmark for apparel. Average order value increased 37% through bundling and upsell strategy. Conversion rate landed at 3.83%, significantly above the ecommerce average.

More importantly, the brand built a paid media engine that could scale — not just spike.

Key Takeaway

"AOV is often the most underrated lever in ecommerce. Sell the same number of units but put the right offer in front of the right customer — and the economics of every campaign change."
